Personal 14-Allergen Guard on Every Product Card

You do not need an account or cloud profile to check ingredients while shopping. Your allergen selection is stored exclusively on your device inside encrypted Android Keystore storage.

  • Proactive setup: Choose any of the 14 regulated EU allergen groups (gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soybeans, milk, nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites, lupin, molluscs) in Settings or directly on any product card.
  • Batch Collector supermarket check: Scan multiple groceries in a row; each collected item immediately highlights matched personal allergens and Nutri-Score in your list.
  • Open product knowledge bases: In Normal Mode, EAN and UPC codes query Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts, and Open Pet Food Facts in parallel (with UPCItemDB fallback and Open Library for ISBN books).
  • Pharma & GS1 DataMatrix: Offline decoding of GTIN, expiry dates, batch numbers, and US FDA National Drug Code (NDC) medicine labels.

3-Tier Precision Recall Matching Across 7 Official Sources

Generic keyword searches flood shoppers with false alarms for unrelated chocolate, cheese, or skincare products. Secure QR Scanner combines device locale and GS1 country prefixes with a deterministic 3-tier confidence engine across EU Safety Gate, EU RASFF, lebensmittelwarnung.de, openFDA (food & drug enforcement), US CPSC, RappelConso, and UK FSA.

How Recall Confidence Levels Protect Against False Positives
Confidence Tier Matching Criterion App Behavior
Exact Identifier Digit-boundary exact match on GTIN, EAN, UPC, PZN, or NDC (required for EU RASFF) High-visibility red recall alert with authority risk reason
Brand + Product Match Normalized brand plus at least two specific product core tokens (generic nouns excluded) Amber caution notice prompting batch/lot verification
Generic Keyword Match Single category noun without brand or barcode match Automatically suppressed to eliminate noisy false warnings
Local Checks Mode compatible: Barcode formats, GS1 expiry dates, batch numbers, and local history stay readable 100% offline. In Local Mode, outbound product and recall API calls remain disabled.

Kurzüberblick auf Deutsch: Allergen- & Rückruf-Scanner ohne Werbung

Suchen Sie einen Allergen-Barcode-Scanner ohne Werbung und Konto? Secure QR Scanner prüft Lebensmittel-Barcodes über Open Food Facts (sowie Kosmetik und Haustierfutter), warnt direkt vor Ihren persönlich gespeicherten 14 EU-Allergenen (auch im Serien-Scan beim Einkauf) und gleicht Produkte treffsicher mit amtlichen Rückruf-Datenbanken wie lebensmittelwarnung.de, EU Safety Gate, EU RASFF, RappelConso, UK FSA, openFDA und CPSC ab.

  • Kein Nutzerkonto, keine Tracker: Ihr Allergen-Profil verlässt niemals unverschlüsselt Ihr Gerät.
  • Keine Fehlalarm-Flut: Exakte GTIN/EAN/PZN-Treffer werden klar von reinen Marken-/Produkthinweisen („bitte Charge prüfen“) getrennt; unspezifische 1-Wort-Treffer werden herausgefiltert.
