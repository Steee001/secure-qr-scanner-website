What is Quishing? (QR Phishing Guide)

Quishing (a combination of "QR code" and "phishing") is a social engineering attack where fraudsters replace legitimate QR codes with deceptive ones designed to steal passwords, credentials, or payment details.

Common Quishing Scenarios

  • Parking meters & EV charging stations: Criminals paste physical sticker overlays over legitimate payment QR codes, redirecting drivers to cloned payment pages.
  • Fake package delivery notices & SMS: Letters or text messages claiming a missed delivery with a QR code or short link that requests payment of fake customs fees.
  • Restaurant table menus: Fraudulent stickers placed over tabletop codes that prompt diners to enter credit card numbers or download malicious APK files.
  • Utility bills & invoices: Bogus invoices sent by mail or email containing QR codes leading to unauthorized bank transfer requests.

How Secure QR Scanner Protects You

  • Offline Heuristics: Detects look-alike letters (punycode / homograph attacks) and embedded user credentials.

Tips for Scanning Safely

  1. Check for stickers: Always inspect whether a QR code on a parking meter or public poster has a sticker pasted over the original surface.
  2. Review before clicking: Use a scanner that shows the full destination URL and waits for your confirmation instead of opening your browser automatically.
  3. Check SMS links: If you receive a suspicious text or chat link, use Android's Share feature to send it to Check link in Secure QR Scanner before opening it.
