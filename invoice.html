Invoice Quishing & Payment Verification

Inspect GiroCode, QR-Bill & Payment QR Codes Offline Before Paying

Attackers swap invoice QR stickers or embed fake web checkout links inside bills. Secure QR Scanner parses regional payment standards locally on-device, checks IBAN math, and cross-checks printed invoice text against the QR payload.

Catch Tampered Bills Before Opening Any Banking App

Instead of blindly handing raw QR text to an external app or browser, every payment payload stops in a structured review dialog completely offline:

  • Clear recipient & amount breakdown: Displays payee name, account / IBAN, currency, transfer amount, and payment reference in plain text.
  • IBAN & FEBRABAN check-digit verification: Validates international IBAN mod-97 checksums and Brazilian Boleto bank-slip check digits locally before you copy details to your banking app.
  • Optical Invoice OCR Cross-Check: Compare the QR code on a paper letter or PDF screen directly against nearby printed OCR text to spot IBAN or amount discrepancies caused by pasted fraud stickers.
  • Fake link separation: If a billing notice hides a phishing https:// portal instead of an authentic structured payment QR format, it is routed through full redirect resolution and phishing inspection.
