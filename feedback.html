Privacy Protection: Secure QR Scanner has zero tracking and never collects your scan history. Because GitHub issue reports are public, never include confidential Wi-Fi passwords, 2FA secret keys, personal banking credentials, or private URLs with tokens. For domain issues, provide only the domain name (e.g. example.com).
