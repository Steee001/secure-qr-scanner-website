Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Secure QR Scanner is designed with privacy and data protection as core principles. The app has no user accounts, requires no registration, and displays zero advertisements.

1. Operating Modes

Normal Mode (Default): Provides full real-time security checking, Safe Browsing verification, redirect resolution, product barcode details, and recall checks.

Optional Local Checks Mode: Blocks automatic network requests by this app (security feed sync, redirects, online checks, product/recall lookups, image downloads, and diagnostics reporting). Scanned links are evaluated solely against the locally cached threat database and offline phishing heuristics. Enabling or disabling local mode requires a complete process restart (Android App Info → Force stop, then reopen).

2. Link Security & Threat Verification

In Normal Mode, web links (HTTP/HTTPS) are evaluated before opening:

  • Redirect Resolution: The app contacts the initial host and follows intermediate redirect hops (e.g., bit.ly, t.co) to reveal the final destination domain. Contacted servers receive standard HTTP requests containing your IP address.
  • Google Safe Browsing: URL-derived 4-byte SHA-256 hash prefixes are checked on-device against a locally synchronized Google Safe Browsing prefix database (refreshed periodically over unmetered Wi-Fi in Normal Mode), querying Google only with matching 4-byte prefixes for confirmation. Prior to the first full offline database synchronization, a fallback check may query the Google Safe Browsing Lookup API.
  • Offline Phishing Heuristics: Offline pattern matching flags punycode tricks, look-alike domains, and suspicious subdomain nesting directly on your device.

3. Product Barcodes & Recalls

Scanning a product barcode (EAN/UPC) in Normal Mode queries Open Food Facts and affiliated databases (Open Products Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Pet Food Facts) in parallel, with UPCItemDB queried only as a fallback when no match is found, and loads available product thumbnail images into a local cache tied to your history retention settings. For book barcodes (ISBN-13 beginning with 978 or 979), the app queries the Open Library API (openlibrary.org) to retrieve book title, author, and cover images. If a product is identified, the app checks official locale-relevant recall databases (EU Safety Gate, EU RASFF, openFDA, CPSC, lebensmittelwarnung.de, RappelConso, and UK FSA), including periodic unmetered Wi-Fi synchronization of the public lebensmittelwarnung.de recall snapshot for instant local matching. Local mode makes no outbound lookups and uses only previously cached product and recall data.

4. Document Scanning & Text OCR

Barcode decoding and text recognition (Latin and Devanagari) run on-device using bundled ML Kit models (with outbound SDK telemetry suppressed in Local Mode). Document scanning utilizes the Google Play services ML Kit document scanner (requiring explicit confirmation when Local Mode is active). Multi-page PDF assembly and OCR text extraction are performed entirely on your device; scanned document pages, temporary images, and exported PDFs remain in local device storage and are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud.

5. Local Data Storage & Encryption

Your scan history and cached product details are encrypted on-device using AES-256-GCM via the Android Keystore, with system cloud backups disabled. Nobody else—including the app developer—has access to your history. Clearing or disabling your scan history in Settings also clears locally cached product metadata and images.

6. Crash Diagnostics & Technical Identifiers

In Normal Mode, crash reporting uses Firebase Crashlytics and is enabled by default to ensure app stability across Android versions. You can disable Crash Diagnostics at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Crash diagnostics (effective upon restarting the app). In Local Mode, Firebase Crashlytics is not initialized and no reports are transmitted.

Crash reports may include technical stack traces, OS and device models, and installation identifiers. The app never includes scanned URLs, barcodes, Wi-Fi credentials, 2FA secrets, contacts, or OCR text in Crashlytics reports.

7. Contact & Security Disclosures

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or your data rights, please contact the developer via our Feedback page or the support link on the Google Play Store listing. Security researchers can report vulnerabilities confidentially via our GitHub Security Advisory channel (RFC 9116 reference available at /.well-known/security.txt).
